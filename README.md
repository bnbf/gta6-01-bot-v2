LUMEN BAY: Neon Heat — open-world crime in a neon coastal city: 7 story missions, police helicopter, 4 weapons, car radio, shops, safehouse, NOS, saves. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

An original 2D canvas top-down open-world crime game. Golden dusk falls into neon night over an original coastal city — glowing neon signs, street lamps, headlights, and a light pool around the player. Play 7 story missions (First Wheels, Neon Courier, Protection Money, Heat Wave, The Big Score, Midnight Race, King of the Bay), outrun a police helicopter from 4 wanted stars, switch between 4 weapons, tune the car radio to 2 procedural synthwave stations, shop at gun stores and garages, rest at the safehouse, and pull off drive-by shootings with NOS. A live minimap, wanted stars, and localStorage saves round it out. Original city, no Rockstar content.

## Run it

```sh
cd game
python3 -m http.server 8123
# open http://localhost:8123 in a browser
# self-test: http://localhost:8123/?test=1
```
